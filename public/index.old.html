PROBABILITY CALIBRATION CHRONOLOGICAL OUT-OF-SAMPLE

Temperature scaling is fitted on the first 60% of chronological out-of-fold forecasts and evaluated on the later 40%. Metrics: multiclass log-loss, Brier score, top-label expected calibration error (ECE), maximum calibration error (MCE), and classwise ECE. If the fitted temperature hits a search boundary or the sample is too small, calibration is not applied. Calibration quality is not predictive edge.
